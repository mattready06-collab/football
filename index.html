Football

Football is a game involving two seperate teams. The end goal of the game is to score points whether by touchdowns or by field goals. The players must have strength, agility, and teamwork to perform the best. The team with the most points by the end of the 60 minute clock wins.

Teamwork

Teamwork is one of the most important parts of a football game. There is a job for each player, and it only becomes possible for a team to win because of the good performance of all the players on the field. Communication and cooperation between players is a must.

Other lessons that can be gained from football are stuff that extends beyond the sport itself. Some of those lessons that may be learned are responsibility, respect, and perserverance.

Practice and Game Day

Practice allows preparation for the players for their game. They learn how to execute their plays and condition themselves while practicing.

Every player has a role to execute on gameday. The responsibility for the offense is to score points. The quaterback is in command of the offense while the offensive line blocks for him, the runningback can block, run, or catch the ball, and the recievers catch the ball. The defense is to stop the opponents offense from scoring. The middle linebacker is normally in charge of the defense. The fans contribute to the game by cheering as loud as possible to disrupt the flow of the other team.

  • listen to your coaches.
  • Work hard during practice.
  • Respect your teammates and opponents.
  • Learn from mistakes.
